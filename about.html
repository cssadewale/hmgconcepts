About HMG Concepts

The story behind a Nigerian educational brand built on deliberate learning, authentic teaching, and the conviction that technology — applied wisely — can close real gaps.

Our Origin

Where It All Started

HMG Concepts — full name, His Marvellous Grace Educational Consult — was founded in 2015 by Adewale Samson Adeagbo, a secondary-school teacher with a burning question: why were Nigerian students being failed by a system that could be fixed?

The answer wasn't to complain. It was to build. What began as a one-man tutoring operation has grown into a full brand with four living arms — Academy, Technologies, Media, and Gospel.

Through it all, the philosophy has never changed: Learning Deliberately. Teaching Authentically.

“I always wanted to solve classroom problems with technology. That drive is what led me to study Computer Science Education — and it's what still drives every decision we make at HMG Concepts today.”

— Adewale Samson Adeagbo, Founder
🎯

Our Mission

To give every Nigerian learner access to quality, deliberate, forward-looking education — and to build the tools, content, and outreach that make that access real, wherever they are.

🔭

Our Vision

An ecosystem where education, technology, media, and faith reinforce one another — solving real problems for real Nigerian communities with integrity and excellence.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Six convictions that shape every lesson, every tool, every piece of content, and every outreach across the HMG ecosystem.

01

Deliberate Learning

We don't teach for passing exams alone. We teach for understanding — building students who can think, not just recall.

02

Authentic Teaching

Our teachers bring real experience, not just qualifications. Every lesson is grounded in what actually works in the classroom.

03

Technology as a Tool

We leverage technology deliberately — to expand access, improve outcomes, and solve problems that have long gone unsolved.

04

Access for All

Quality education should not be a privilege. HMG is virtual by design, so geography and circumstance are never barriers.

05

Community Impact

We build for Nigeria. Every tool, lesson, and piece of content is made with the Nigerian learner's context in mind.

06

Continuous Growth

We practise what we preach. HMG Concepts is a learning organisation — always upskilling, always improving, always evolving.

Our Journey

Milestones That Shaped Us

2015

HMG Concepts is founded

His Marvellous Grace Educational Consult begins as a one-man tutoring operation with a big conviction.

2023

Academy formalised & CS Education degree

HMG Academy grows into a structured virtual institution as the founder earns a B.Sc.(Ed) in Computer Science Education.

2024–25

The technology leap

3MTT unlocks data science capability; CBT Pro and the first EdTech tools go live in real classrooms.

2026

Four living arms

Technologies, Media, and Gospel join Academy as fully live subsidiaries — one brand, four missions.

“His Marvellous Grace” is not just a name — it is a conviction. Integrity, authentic service, and community impact are not aspirational here. They are operational.
