Questions & Answers

Everything you might want to know about HMG Concepts and its four arms. Still unsure? We're one message away.

What exactly is HMG Concepts?

HMG Concepts — full name His Marvellous Grace Educational Consult — is a Nigerian education and technology brand founded in 2015 by Adewale Samson Adeagbo. It operates four live arms: HMG Academy (virtual schooling), HMG Technologies (AI-augmented tools & data), HMG Media (content & visibility), and HMG Gospel (Christ-centred digital outreach).

How is HMG Academy different from a regular online tutor?

HMG Academy is a full virtual institution, not a single-tutor arrangement. It has a team of seasoned teachers covering all subjects and levels — Nursery through Secondary — and prepares students for Nigerian exams (WAEC, NECO, GCE, BECE, UTME) and international ones (IGCSE, IELTS, JUPEB, SAT).

How do I enrol or register a student?

Head to hmgacademy.pages.dev and use the Enrol/Register page, or simply message us on WhatsApp (+234 810 086 6322) and our team will walk you through it.

Is the CBT Pro exam platform really free?

Yes. CBT Pro was deliberately built on a ₦0 budget and is free for schools and students. It includes timed exams, anti-cheat, auto-marking, a teacher analytics dashboard, and certificate generation.

What does HMG Technologies build, and for whom?

Custom digital tools for Nigerian businesses, schools, NGOs, and churches — CBT systems, data dashboards (Power BI, Tableau, Streamlit), AI-augmented web apps, machine-learning tools, and 11 browser-based data-tool simulators, plus training and consultancy. Request a quote on the Technologies site.

How much does HMG Media cost?

Media offers guide packages: Visibility Starter ₦50k+, Content Engine ₦120k+, and Full Media Support (custom). Final pricing depends on scope — start a project on the Media site.

What is HMG Gospel about?

HMG Gospel is the faith arm — Christ-centred digital outreach and discipleship. Its pillars include dramavangelism, techvangelism, teaching content, podcasts, ebooks, and church/community support. “Every platform a pulpit, every tool a testimony.”

Do your platforms use paid AI APIs?

No. By design, our tools avoid paid AI API dependence and unnecessary recurring costs. This keeps everything cost-effective and sustainable — a core HMG discipline.

Where are you based, and do you work remotely?

We're based in Lagos / Ogun State, Nigeria, and because the model is virtual-first, we serve students and clients anywhere in Nigeria — and remote collaborations are welcome.

How do I partner or collaborate with HMG?

Use the contact form (it opens a ready-to-send WhatsApp message) or reach us directly at hismarvellousgrace@gmail.com. For tech partnerships: buildingmyictcareer@gmail.com.
