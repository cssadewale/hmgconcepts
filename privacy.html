Transparency

Privacy & Storage

We keep this simple, like everything else at HMG — honest, lightweight, and respectful of your data.

What we store

This website uses only essential browser storage (localStorage) to remember your theme choice and announcement-bar dismissal. This data never leaves your device.

What we don't do

No tracking cookies. No third-party analytics. No advertising pixels. No selling or sharing of personal data. We deliberately avoid unnecessary recurring tools and do not use paid AI APIs — consistent with the wider HMG cost-discipline approach.

The enquiry & newsletter forms

Our forms do not run a hidden backend. The enquiry form assembles your details into a WhatsApp message that you choose to send; the newsletter opens your email app to confirm. Nothing is stored on a server by this site.

Offline support (PWA)

This site can be installed and works offline using a service worker that caches pages on your device only. You can clear it any time from your browser settings.
